# Jared C. Tate

**Occupation:** Blockchain Developer, Author
Known for: Founding the DigiByte Blockchain

**Early Life and Education**
Jared C. Tate was born and raised in the United States. From a young age, he exhibited a keen interest in technology and computer science. He pursued his higher education in computer science, which laid the foundation for his future endeavors in the blockchain industry.

**Career**
Jared Tate is best known as the founder of the DigiByte blockchain, a public, rapidly growing, and highly decentralized blockchain. DigiByte was officially launched on January 10, 20141. Tate’s vision was to create a more secure, faster, and decentralized blockchain compared to existing ones like Bitcoin and Litecoin.

**DigiByte Blockchain**
The DigiByte blockchain is notable for its speed and security. It uses five different cryptographic algorithms to enhance security and prevent mining centralization1. DigiByte blocks occur every 15 seconds, making it 40 times faster than Bitcoin2. Over the years, DigiByte has introduced several innovations, including DigiShield and MultiAlgo, which have set it apart in the blockchain space.

**Publications**
In addition to his work on DigiByte, Jared Tate is also an author. He wrote “Blockchain 2035,” a book that explores the potential applications of blockchain technology and its future impact3. This publication has contributed to his reputation as a thought leader in the blockchain community.

**Personal Life**
Jared Tate is known for his dedication to the principles of decentralization and open-source development. He continues to be actively involved in the DigiByte community and advocates for the broader adoption of blockchain technology.

**Legacy**
Jared Tate’s contributions to the blockchain industry have been significant. Through DigiByte, he has demonstrated the potential of blockchain technology to create secure, fast, and decentralized digital ecosystems. His work continues to inspire new developments and innovations in the field.
